- `Magnanimous Telecom` is the telecom service-provider identity.
- `God Matters` is the owner name for Magnanimous Telecom.
- **Do not add ™, ®, or the word “Trademark” to `God Matters` unless the owner explicitly changes this rule later.**

## Telecom business lock

The ownership statement does not change the existing retail-service lock:

- white-label telecom: disabled

- customer number resale: disabled
- provider-brand override by external vendors: disabled

Customers purchase telecom service from Magnanimous Telecom. They do not receive a clone, resale version, or carrier-building version of the Magnanimous Telecom infrastructure.

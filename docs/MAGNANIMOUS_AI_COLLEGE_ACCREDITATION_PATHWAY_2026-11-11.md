# Magnanimous AI College - Philippine Degree Recognition Pathway
Research date: 11 November 2026\nStatus: Planning and partner outreach required. No accreditation or degree-awarding authority is claimed.

## Recommended route
Build Magnanimous AI as the learning platform, instructional technology, AI lab and student-support layer. Partner with an existing Philippine Higher Education Institution (HEI) that already has the relevant CHED-recognized undergraduate program. The HEI—not Magnanimous AI alone—must control admissions, academic governance, curriculum approval, faculty appointments, assessment, official student records, transcripts and degree issuance.

The most practical initial program to explore is **Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) with an AI specialization/track**, subject to the partner's academic approval and CHED requirements. Do not advertise a standalone BS Artificial Intelligence degree unless CHED authorizes that exact program.

## Governing CHED requirements
### 1. Existing recognized degree program is required before ODeL application
CHED Memorandum Order No. 12, series of 2025 (ODeL) states that only HEIs with CHED-recognized programs (Government Recognition or Certificate of Program Compliance), a satisfactory ODeL capability self-assessment (no score below 2 in any item), and at least 51% of the program's course units delivered through ODeL may apply for authority to offer programs via ODeL.

### 2. Online delivery is possible, but it must be authorized
CMO No. 12, s. 2025 allows undergraduate programs to be offered 100% through ODeL when the HEI demonstrates the capacity to deliver the program and meet the program's required competencies. Non-PRC-regulated fields such as IT may be fully online if the HEI demonstrates sufficient capability. Required labs, hands-on courses, OJT, internships and NSTP still need compliant delivery arrangements.

### 3. Institutional readiness and academic quality
The HEI must provide, among other things:
- An approved ODeL Manual of Operations and institutional mission, budget and policies supporting ODeL.
- An accountable organizational structure and academic oversight.
- An ODeL head/key person with at least 100 hours of relevant ODeL training, preferably six months of course-development experience or at least one course developed and delivered, and at least two years of management experience.
- An internal quality-assurance system and continuous self-evaluation.
- Self-instructional materials for every course, equivalent in depth and content to classroom delivery, with pilot testing and peer review.
- Qualified faculty and appropriate student, technical, library, infrastructure and support services.
- Curriculum compliance with the relevant CHED Policies, Standards and Guidelines (PSG).

### 4. Application timing and approvals
- Only a qualifying HEI may apply, at least **one year before** the intended program offering.
- The application is submitted to the concerned CHED Regional Office with the complete electronic and hardcopy evidence listed in the ODeL Evaluation Form.
- CHEDRO evaluates the program, infrastructure, systems and institutional readiness; a joint evaluation with the Technical Panel for ODeL may be conducted.
- Fully compliant applications are endorsed through the Office of Programs and Standards Development to the Commission en banc for approval.
- Government Authority to offer via ODeL is valid for eight years, subject to monitoring and renewal.
- Public announcements of ODeL program offerings may be made only after CHED authority has been obtained.

### 5. Foreign and overseas students
CMO No. 12, s. 2025 also requires authority under the Transnational Higher Education (TNHE) framework when an ODeL provider/HEI intends to accept students abroad or outside the Philippines. Foreign-student enrollment limits and related permissions must also be observed. Do not assume a Philippines-only ODeL approval authorizes overseas enrolment.

## Implementation sequence
1. Request a NORSU exploratory meeting with the Office of the Vice President for Academic Affairs and the BSIT/BSCS program leadership. Ask whether NORSU is open to an AI-specialization partnership using Magnanimous AI as the learning and lab platform.
2. Ask NORSU to confirm its current BSIT COPC scope, campus coverage, curriculum authority, and whether it already has CHED ODeL Government Authority for BSIT. Do not infer ODeL approval from an existing BSIT COPC or AACCUP accreditation.
3. Ask CHEDRO-NIR to confirm the exact ODeL application process, timing, supporting documents, and whether the proposed AI track is a curriculum revision or a new program/major requiring additional approval.
4. Draft an MOA/MOU defining academic authority, faculty, intellectual property, data protection, student records, assessment, grievance/appeal handling, fees, refunds, liability, platform uptime, exit/teach-out protection, and public use of each institution's name.
5. Co-design the BSIT AI track under CMO No. 25, s. 2015 and the partner's approved curriculum. Include mathematics, computing fundamentals, software engineering, data management, AI/ML, cybersecurity, ethics, cloud systems, capstone, and practical competencies as the partner and CHED require.
6. Build the ODeL course materials, faculty training, student support, labs, accessibility, QA evidence, records retention, and disaster-recovery controls to the partner's and CHED's standards.
7. Submit the ODeL application at least one year before the intended launch. Do not announce or enroll students in the degree program until the relevant CHED authority is granted.
8. Until then, keep the Magnanimous Open Campus clearly labelled **non-degree / non-credit learning**. It may offer transparent learning resources and skills practice, but it must not imply a recognized degree, accredited college, CHED approval, credit transfer, or guaranteed employment.

## Decision and compliance guardrails
- NORSU is a **prospective partner**, not a confirmed partner.
- Existing BSIT COPC/AACCUP status does not automatically extend to a new AI major, new curriculum or online delivery mode.
- A platform certificate is not a bachelor's degree and must never be represented as CHED-recognized.
- No tuition, paid enrollment, degree claim, official transcript or degree issuance until the partner's authority, written agreement and required CHED approvals are verified.
- Keep the user's free-first principle: use NORSU/CHED's existing infrastructure and an affordable partnership model before considering a new standalone HEI.

# Magnanimous Enterprise Sales Operations

## Purpose
Turn the deployed Enterprise Command, CX Command and BPO Operations layers into a disciplined enterprise sales-to-delivery workflow without creating owner-funded variable-cost exposure or unauthorized access to customer systems.

## Operating funnel
1. Identify a public buying signal: contact-center transformation, IVR/CCaaS migration, BPO procurement, workforce/quality modernization, support scaling or AI automation.
2. Create one canonical enterprise account. Deduplicate by normalized company name + website/domain before creating another record.
3. Create an opportunity with source URL/evidence, stage, estimated value, probability, next action and due date.
4. Run qualification: business problem, channels, seats/volume, geography, hours, languages, required integrations, data classification, security/compliance and procurement path.
5. Produce a scoped proposal/SOW. Do not claim an integration is live until the customer has supplied authorization and credentials and it has passed validation.
6. Contract and implementation. Keep metered carrier/AI/avatar/video services within included allowance or customer-funded prepaid usage.
7. Launch the client as a segregated BPO/CX program with SLA, QA, WFM, routing, audit and escalation controls.
8. Track revenue, direct usage cost and gross-margin target. The 20% value is a target/guardrail, not a profit guarantee.

## Target qualification score (100)
- Active buying/procurement signal: 25
- Contact-center/BPO/AI automation fit: 20
- Multi-channel or material service volume: 15
- Clear executive/operations owner: 10
- Integration feasibility: 10
- Budget/contracting evidence: 10
- Security/compliance fit: 10

Prioritize >=70. Research 50-69. Archive <50 unless a new signal appears.

## Required opportunity evidence
Store the public source URL, source date, signal type and a short factual summary. Never infer that a company is a customer merely because it has a relevant job opening or RFP.

## Procurement-ready package
Maintain reusable artifacts for: company overview, capability matrix, architecture, service catalog, SLA schedule, pricing assumptions, implementation plan, security questionnaire response, privacy/data-processing addendum checklist, business continuity/disaster recovery, incident escalation, QA/WFM model, accessibility, subcontractor/provider list and references/case studies when real references exist.

## Regulated clients
Banks, insurers, healthcare and public-sector programs default to restricted data classification. Require least-privilege access, tenant/client separation, audit logging, data minimization, retention rules, secrets in the protected credential system, and documented authorization before private-system access. Never request or store customer passwords, OTPs or full payment-card authentication data in ordinary support workflows.

## Outreach controls
Public research and CRM preparation may be automated. External outreach must use truthful identity, legitimate recipient/source data, opt-out handling and applicable anti-spam/telemarketing rules. Telephone campaigns continue to require consent/DNC/quiet-hour/rate controls. Do not enable unrestricted predictive robodialing.

## Free-first economics
Free browser WebRTC and free/quota AI remain the default where suitable. Metered PSTN, premium AI, avatars and generated video must be paid by plan allowance, prepaid customer credits, customer-owned provider accounts, or an approved contract budget. Never silently convert premium usage into unlimited owner-funded spend.

## Current target-list hygiene
Apollo Free blocks the prospect-search APIs. Public buying-signal research can still populate Magnanimous Enterprise Command. Apollo records should be deduplicated before creation where possible; when the connector cannot delete duplicates, only the canonical record belongs in the active Magnanimous Enterprise Targets list.

- SEC corporate registration;
- BIR registration/tax obligations;
- Bayawan Mayor's/Business Permit and local clearances;
- NTC VAS/VoIP/mobile/internet/other telecom authority;

- fire/occupancy/sanitary/zoning/barangay/police clearances;

- any PEZA/BOI/FIRB incentive registration.

Trying to merge these into one government license or one government payment would create legal risk.

- Starting April 7, 2025, domestic stock corporations other than lending/financing companies are processed through SEC ZERO.

## NTC / telecom legal stack

### Phase 1 — own Call Center
The Call Center may use authorized carrier services while NTC confirms whether internal-only telecom use requires separate registration. No public telecom resale is enabled merely because the software works.

### Phase 2 — B2B telecom for other call centers
Before selling hosted VoIP/SIP/public telecom service:
- obtain the NTC classification/registration directed by Region VII;
- execute the required authorized-provider/reseller agreement;
- complete Form NTC 1-20 package;
- submit service description, equipment list, block diagram/system description and rates;
- if VoIP Reseller remains the chosen route, obtain the current ₱1,000,000 performance/surety bond;
- complete billing/CDR/fraud/spend/abuse controls.

### Phase 3 — public/global telephone, SIM/eSIM, data and internet
Before public activation:
- obtain NTC classification/authority for the actual service model;
- execute Philippine host-carrier / reseller / VNO / MVNO or other approved agreement;
- establish SIM-registration/KYC responsibility;
- obtain provider-issued real production SIM/eSIM profiles;
- verify country/service eligibility;
- verify origin wholesale prices and fair-use/spend policy;
- prove real subscriber connectivity;
- prove independent backup before advertising resilient multi-network service;
- obtain numbering/porting/public-telecom authority where applicable.

No software flag or provider credential may substitute for this evidence.

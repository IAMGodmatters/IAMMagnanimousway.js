## Netwalk safety contract

The Netwalk pattern is absorbed as a Magnanimous network-observability specification, but **network survey execution is not marked live until a real local/private-network bridge exists**.

Required guarantees:

- surveyed device commands are read-only and must pass an enforced vendor allowlist;
- credentials never enter Magnanimous chat/model context;
- secrets are collected only through an approved local credential UI or secret manager;
- no subnet sweep without recorded owner authorization and exact scope;
- no configuration changes, counter clearing, service restarts or reboots during survey;
- configuration exports remain local and outside reports/model context;
- unreachable, unidentified, skipped and out-of-scope devices remain visible coverage results;
- every finding needs evidence, confidence, severity and a concrete recommendation;
- topology and report outputs derive from one structured source-of-truth record;
- public reports hide sensitive operational detail;
- incomplete coverage is always disclosed.

# Magnanimous Call Center + Telecom — Unified Philippine Launch Plan

Last updated: 2026-09-26

## Permanent product rule

Magnanimous Call Center and Magnanimous Telecom are one operating system.

- The Call Center is the first anchor customer of Magnanimous Telecom.
- Magnanimous Telecom supplies the owned PBX/SIP/WebRTC/routing, PSTN/mobile upstreams, numbers, recording transport and later SIM/eSIM/mobile-data capability used by the Call Center.
- Magnanimous Call Center supplies the first real workload, agent operations, QA/WFM, CRM and customer-service use case that proves the telecom layer.
- Neither side may be called fully production-live when its required legal, carrier, privacy, labor, security or technical proof is missing.
- Third-party carriers remain replaceable infrastructure beneath Magnanimous AI / Magnanimous Telecom.

## Commercial expansion model

### Phase 1 — Magnanimous-owned Call Center / BPO

Initial use:
- inbound/outbound customer service;
- AI receptionist and routing;
- browser/WebRTC agent calling;
- CRM, scripts, QA, workforce management and recording/transcription with lawful notice/consent;
- PSTN/mobile connectivity obtained from authorized upstream carriers.

Regulatory/compliance gates before customer production:
- Philippine legal entity;
- BIR and local business permits;
- employer registrations and labor compliance when employees are hired;
- DOLE Establishment Report System registration after the business permit/TIN/SSS information exists;
- Data Protection Officer and National Privacy Commission registration/filing as required;
- privacy notices, client data-processing agreement, retention/security controls and lawful recording notice/consent;
- carrier/BYOC account or authorized host transport for external PSTN/mobile traffic;
- real browser/media/call-routing verification.

NTC classification for internal-only telecom use remains subject to NTC Region VII written confirmation. No public VoIP/telecom resale is implied by Phase 1.

## Phase 2 — Low-cost B2B telecom for other Philippine call centers

Products:
- hosted VoIP/SIP;
- numbers where lawfully obtained through authorized providers;
- call routing, IVR, queues and contact-center connectivity;
- AI receptionist/call-center platform;
- usage/cost controls;
- optional multi-carrier failover.

Required before commercial sale of regulated telecom capability:
- NTC written classification;
- VAS registration and/or VoIP Reseller/Provider registration as directed by NTC;
- executed agreement with a duly authorized/registered provider where the chosen class requires it;
- applicable service description, block diagram, equipment list and rates;
- applicable NTC fees;
- if VoIP Reseller remains the selected route: the current NTC ₱1,000,000 performance-bond requirement or written NTC evidence that a different approved route applies;
- privacy/data-processing contracts for BPO/client traffic;
- verified billing, CDR, fraud, consent, recording, spend and abuse controls.

No B2B public VoIP product may be enabled merely because the software works.

## Phase 3 — Philippine public telephone / SIM / eSIM / mobile data / internet with global reach

Target:
- Magnanimous-branded mobile/telephone/data service;
- Philippine host network;
- global roaming/connectivity;
- SIM/eSIM issued/authenticated by authorized carriers/providers;
- mobile data, voice/SMS/numbers/porting only where contractually and legally authorized;
- independent backup network where actually available and proven.

Required before public activation:
- NTC classification and applicable certificate/authority;
- executed Philippine host-carrier/reseller/VNO/MVNO or other approved agreement;
- country/service eligibility;
- SIM Registration Act/KYC allocation;
- provider-issued production SIM/eSIM profile/reference;
- verified origin wholesale pricing and fair-use/spend controls;
- real subscriber connectivity evidence in the Philippines;
- required public-telecommunications authority beyond reseller/VNO/MVNO status, if NTC determines it applies (including any CPCN/PA/franchise-related requirement);
- numbering/porting authority where applicable;
- real independent backup proof before advertising resilient multi-network service.

`TELECOM_GLOBAL_MOBILE_LIVE` remains false until these proof gates pass.

## Shared Call Center + Telecom launch gates

The operating system should report three distinct launch states:

1. **CALL CENTER INTERNAL READY**
   - entity/local permits;
   - labor/employer setup applicable to active workforce;
   - NPC/DPO privacy path;
   - lawful recording/data controls;
   - authorized external carrier route;
   - public WebRTC/media proof where required.

2. **B2B CALL-CENTER TELECOM READY**
   - all Call Center Internal gates;
   - NTC authority for selling the telecom service;
   - reseller/provider agreement;
   - applicable bond/fees/rates;
   - metering/billing/fraud/abuse controls.

3. **PUBLIC/GLOBAL TELECOM READY**
   - all B2B gates where relevant;
   - Philippine host-carrier/mobile authority;
   - production SIM/eSIM and real connectivity;
   - pricing/fair-use controls;
   - regulatory evidence for public mobile/internet/telephone scope;
   - real failover evidence before resilience claims.

A lower phase may become operational while a higher phase remains fail-closed, but the customer interface must never expose a higher-phase regulated product as live until its proof is complete.

## BPO / employment / incentive track

- PEZA currently identifies BPO/call-center and software activities as eligible IT Service activities for IT Enterprise registration; export-service rules and location requirements must be confirmed for the final operating model.
- Magnanimous has asked PEZA whether BPO + software can be registered together and whether the NTC-regulated telecom arm can remain separate.
- Government/startup funding, DOST, QBO and SBCorp tracks remain parallel to licensing.
- Funding eligibility must never be presented as regulatory authority.

## Privacy track

National Privacy Commission registration is treated as a launch dependency for the call-center data systems when registration is required.

Expected data-processing systems include:
- CRM/customer support;
- call recording/transcription;
- AI QA/profiling/lead scoring;
- employee/agent HR;
- telecom subscriber/account data.

The owner platform must distinguish PIC responsibilities for Magnanimous' own customers/employees from PIP responsibilities when Magnanimous processes a client's data.

## Employment track

Once the Philippine entity, business permit, TIN and SSS employer details exist:
- register the establishment with DOLE's Establishment Report System;
- complete employer registrations;
- maintain wage/hours/benefits and required establishment reports;
- apply applicable night-work, occupational-safety and workplace rules to call-center shifts.

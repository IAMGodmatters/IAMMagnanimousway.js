## Current NTC 1-20 application information to prepare

The public Form NTC 1-20 requests applicant information including:
- applicant/legal entity name;
- complete Philippine address;
- contact number;
- email;
- entity type;
- existing CPCN/PA/CA or COR information if applicable;
- requested category (VAS / PCSOTSP / VoIP Provider / VoIP Reseller);
- declaration signed by applicant or duly authorized representative.

Do not fabricate any blank. The final filing must use the actual Philippine entity and authorized signatory.

## Current published VoIP Provider requirements

The NTC Citizen's Charter currently lists, among other items:
- accomplished NTC 1-20;
- description of proposed VoIP services;
- equipment/material list;
- functional block diagram/system configuration and brief description;
- valid interconnection agreement and/or valid leased-facilities agreement with duly authorized network provider(s);
- valid SEC or DTI registration;
- paid-up capital of at least PHP 10,000,000 for the provider path;
- latest audited financial statement;
- schedule of service rates;
- cities/municipalities where service will be offered;
- PHP 5,000,000 performance bond for the provider path.

These are public checklist items, not evidence that Magnanimous currently satisfies them.

## Published VoIP Reseller requirements requiring current NTC confirmation

The historical NTC 3-11-2005 reseller guideline states requirements including:
- SEC/DTI registration with at least 60% Filipino ownership;
- certified reseller agreement with a duly registered VoIP service provider;
- filing fee of PHP 180;
- registration fee of PHP 5,000/year;
- PHP 1,000,000 performance bond.

Because rules, fees and implementation can change, these values are retained as a regulatory research baseline only. NTC Region VII has been asked to confirm the currently applicable requirements before any payment, bond or filing.

## SIM/eSIM regulatory boundary

The SIM Registration Act IRR places end-user SIM registration with the respective Public Telecommunications Entities (PTEs). The exact allocation of SIM-registration, subscriber/KYC, retention, privacy and reporting duties in a branded host-network/MVNO/reseller arrangement must be defined by the host agreement and NTC classification.

Magnanimous therefore does not treat an eSIM API or roaming profile as authority to issue Philippine public mobile service independently.

## Filing package structure

When NTC confirms classification, assemble:

1. **Entity documents**
   - Philippine SEC/DTI registration as applicable;
   - Articles/By-Laws or business registration;
   - authorized-signatory evidence;
   - ownership/capital evidence if required;
   - latest audited financial statements if required.

2. **Service description**
   - Magnanimous Telecom product description;
   - app/VoIP/contact-center/mobile scope;
   - customer support and complaints path;
   - proposed cities/municipalities.

3. **Technical package**
   - functional block diagram;
   - Asterisk/SIP/control-plane description;
   - upstream interconnection boundary;
   - mobile/eSIM host-network boundary;
   - security and secret-management description;
   - billing/usage/fraud/fair-use controls;
   - emergency-calling truth boundary.

4. **Network/commercial evidence**
   - interconnection/leased-facility/reseller/host-network agreement as required;
   - carrier/MNO/MVNO/eSIM partner agreement;
   - provider authority/evidence references.

5. **Commercial/customer materials**
   - schedule of service rates;
   - terms of service;
   - privacy/SIM-registration disclosures;
   - fair-use policy;
   - emergency-service disclosures.

6. **Financial/security instruments**
   - filing/registration fees only after NTC confirms current amount;
   - required performance bond only after NTC confirms category/current amount;
   - no payment is initiated without explicit authorization.

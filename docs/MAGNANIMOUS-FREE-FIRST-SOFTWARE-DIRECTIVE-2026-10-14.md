# Magnanimous Free-First Software Directive — 2026-10-14

## Directive

Magnanimous AI must prefer free, native and open-source tools before recurring paid software whenever the preferred tool can satisfy the requested outcome. A free tier or free edition may be used when the selected substitute is proprietary, but a paid upgrade is never implied or automatic.

The directive changes tool selection and workflow planning across I AM MAGNANIMOUS WAY. It does not claim that a desktop application is installed, that an external account is connected, or that Magnanimous owns third-party software.

## Native contact-data optimization

ApiVault's Phone category was evaluated for call-center and telecom use. Basic phone validation is better served free-first by the MIT-licensed `libphonenumber-js` package inside the Worker rather than by defaulting to metered/key-gated validation vendors.

Magnanimous now owns a native phone normalization capability for parsing, E.164 formatting and numbering-plan validity checks. It does **not** claim that the number is assigned, active, reachable, owned by the entered person, connected to a particular carrier account, consented for contact, or outside a DNC registry.

## B2B / contact-center email hygiene

DISIFY is independently verified from its own documentation and is used only for its current no-key core checks: email syntax, mail DNS/MX and disposable-address indicators. Premium DISIFY checks are not activated.

The email hygiene result does **not** prove mailbox existence or ownership and does not create consent, outreach permission, DNC compliance, or deliverability guarantees.

## ApiVault clean-room rule

ApiVault remains a discovery source. Its repository currently declares CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, so Magnanimous does not copy its protected catalog implementation or publish a derivative commercial catalog. Candidate APIs are independently re-verified against each provider's current official documentation, authentication, pricing, terms and runtime behavior before activation.

## Action and authorization

Magnanimous may proactively select these preferred tools, generate compatible workflow instructions and route work toward existing authorized surfaces. Installation, login/account connection, paid upgrades, destructive migration, external publishing, messaging/calling, payments, credentials and regulated telecom actions keep their existing permission and legal gates.

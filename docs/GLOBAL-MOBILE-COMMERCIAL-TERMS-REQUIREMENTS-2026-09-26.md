# Magnanimous Telecom — Global Mobile Commercial Terms Requirements

Date: 2026-09-26
Purpose: qualification checklist for any MNO, MVNO, MVNE, eSIM platform, roaming hub or connectivity reseller agreement.

This is an internal commercial/technical acceptance checklist, not a claim that a provider has accepted these terms and not a substitute for legal review of a final contract.

## Required before production

A candidate agreement must clearly identify:

- contracting legal entities;
- the specific right for Magnanimous Telecom to offer the permitted service to end customers under Magnanimous branding;
- countries/territories and customer types covered;
- whether consumer, business, travel, IoT, voice, SMS, phone-number, porting and data use are permitted;
- eSIM/pSIM provisioning rights and API access;
- wholesale fixed charges and all metered/variable charges;
- taxes, surcharges, deposits, minimum commitments and setup fees;
- any prepaid reserve, credit limit or security-deposit requirements;
- usage measurement, dispute and reconciliation rules;
- fair-use, throttling, abuse and hotspot restrictions;
- permanent-roaming restrictions and local-profile requirements;
- KYC, SIM registration, lawful-intercept and other local compliance allocation;
- data-processing/privacy/security obligations;
- SLA, outage escalation and support contacts;
- suspension/termination and cure periods;
- number portability and customer migration/exit rights where numbering is included;
- treatment of unused balances and refunds;
- provider change/termination export obligations;
- API/webhook lifecycle and breaking-change/versioning policy;
- confidentiality and permitted public/provider-brand references.

## Magnanimous business protections

Prefer terms that preserve:

1. **Brand control**
   - Magnanimous Telecom / I AM MAGNANIMOUS WAY™ remains customer-facing.
   - Upstream provider branding is not inserted into ordinary customer surfaces unless legally required.

2. **Provider replaceability**
   - The agreement must not require Magnanimous AI identity, memory, orchestration or customer records to become provider-owned.
   - APIs and data exports should permit orderly provider migration.

3. **Cost defensibility**
   - Current origin pricing must be evidenced by contract, rate card, provider quote or invoice.
   - Retail pricing is generated by Magnanimous from verified origin cost plus the existing 20% uplift rule and required taxes/fees.
   - Variable usage must be bounded by funded exposure or hard spend/usage limits.
   - No silent postpaid overage/fallback is acceptable.

4. **Independent backup**
   - If marketed as redundant, the backup path should be meaningfully independent: different network group, IMSI/profile path, core/roaming route, or other documented failure domain.
   - A contractual promise alone does not mark resilience verified; Magnanimous requires observed failover evidence.

5. **Subscriber portability**
   - Magnanimous should receive reasonable access to customer/subscription usage and lifecycle data needed for support, billing and lawful migration.
   - Where phone numbers are included, port-out rights/processes must be clear.

6. **Security**
   - Raw Ki/OPc/ADM/private SIM authentication material should not be passed into the ordinary Magnanimous application.
   - Provider-issued installation material must be treated as one-time/short-lived where possible and protected from normal logs.
   - API credentials remain server-side encrypted secrets.

7. **No false authority**
   - Provider agreement does not automatically satisfy NTC/FCC/other government authorization.
   - Country availability does not automatically imply emergency-calling, numbering or local-resale authority.

## Commercial red flags

Do not activate production automatically if an agreement includes:

- undefined or changeable pricing without notice;
- unlimited variable charges without hard controls;
- mandatory consumer-facing provider rebranding inconsistent with Magnanimous branding;
- prohibited resale/consumer use for the planned product;
- no lawful path for target-country SIM registration;
- no usable API, provisioning or support path for the promised service;
- restrictions that make ordinary Magnanimous customer support impossible;
- no exit/export path for subscribers or numbers;
- automatic long-term renewal with materially punitive termination conditions;
- a deposit/commitment that has not received explicit payment authorization.

## Evidence fields to record

For every accepted provider offer:
- provider / adapter key;
- commercial agreement reference;
- origin pricing reference;
- country;
- network/failure-domain group;
- valid-from / valid-to;
- monthly origin cost;
- included high-speed data;
- variable data cost;
- funded variable-cost cap;
- mandatory taxes/fees;
- backup eligibility;
- permitted voice/SMS/data/number/porting capabilities;
- observed latency/quality evidence;
- current compliance evidence.

## Go-live rule

A signed commercial agreement is necessary but not sufficient.

Magnanimous Global Mobile remains NOT LIVE until all runtime proof gates pass and the separate environment promotion flag is intentionally enabled after real evidence.

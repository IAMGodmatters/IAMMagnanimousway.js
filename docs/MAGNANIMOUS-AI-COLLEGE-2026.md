## 1. Product purpose

Magnanimous AI College is a practical, online AI learning program built around free, openly accessible learning sources and original Magnanimous instruction.

The college should teach learners to understand, build, test, evaluate, secure and deploy AI systems—not merely copy prompts or use finished chatbots.

The core value sold by Magnanimous is the **guided learning experience**:

- A clear course path matched to each student's starting level and goal.
- Magnanimous AI as the student's persistent tutor, study planner and learning coach.
- Original explanations, exercises, quizzes, rubrics and projects.
- Practical labs and evidence-based project reviews.
- A record of progress and a portfolio of work the student actually completed.
- Completion certificates issued by Magnanimous for its own courses, with clear non-accreditation language.

The original creator's free videos and free curriculum remain accessible without a Magnanimous subscription. A student pays for the added instruction, structure, assessment, progress and project feedback—not for access to somebody else's free video.

## 4. College structure

The product should feel like a real college experience while being honest about its status. It is an independent online academy, not an accredited university or college and not a degree-granting institution.

### Schools

1. **School of AI Literacy** — for people who need to understand and use AI safely without becoming programmers.
2. **School of AI Foundations** — programming, mathematics, data, machine learning and deep learning.
3. **School of AI Engineering** — neural networks, NLP, transformers, LLMs, retrieval and production systems.
4. **School of AI Agents and Automation** — agent loops, tools, MCP, Agent Skills, multi-agent systems, evaluation and orchestration.
5. **School of Responsible AI and Safety** — privacy, security, testing, bias, reliability, cost controls and human oversight.
6. **School of Applied AI** — portfolio projects for business, education, media, customer service and other real-world uses.

### Recommended student pathways

| Path | Audience | Main outcome |
|---|---|---|
| AI Literacy | Beginners and non-programmers | Understand AI, use it responsibly, evaluate outputs and improve everyday workflows |
| AI Builder | Learners who want to make AI applications | Build a small useful AI application using APIs or local models |
| AI Engineer | Programmers and technical learners | Build, evaluate and deploy working AI systems with evidence |
| AI Agent Developer | Developers and advanced learners | Build controlled agents, tool integrations, MCP servers and agent skills |
| AI for Business Operations | Owners, operators and professionals | Design and measure AI-supported workflows with human approval and clear cost boundaries |

### AI Engineer core sequence

The AI Engineering from Scratch curriculum is the core reference spine. Magnanimous should not invent a competing duplicate of its 20 phases; it should guide students through the appropriate source lessons and add its own checkpoints and applied projects.

| Term | Learning block | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Orientation, digital skills, environment setup and placement | Working development environment and baseline assessment |
| 1 | Math foundations, Python, data and classical machine learning | Small ML model, test dataset and explanation of results |
| 2 | Neural networks, deep learning, computer vision and speech | Trained model, evaluation results and documented failure case |
| 3 | NLP, tokenization, transformers and LLM foundations | Small language-model or transformer exercise and explanation |
| 4 | LLM engineering, prompting, retrieval and evaluation | A retrieval/LLM application with a test set and regression checks |
| 5 | Agents, tool use, MCP, Agent Skills and orchestration | A bounded agent that uses approved tools and produces traceable results |
| 6 | Safety, privacy, security, deployment and production operations | Threat model, permission checks, cost controls, deployment and monitoring |
| 7 | Capstone and portfolio | Working application, source repository, test evidence, demo and written reflection |

Term duration should be individualized by the placement assessment and available study hours. The current upstream beginner estimate is roughly 306 hours; it is a guide, not a promised finish date.

### The required lesson cycle

Each college lesson follows a repeatable cycle:

1. **Preview:** Magnanimous states the goal and checks prerequisites.
2. **Watch or read:** The learner uses the free official resource.
3. **Explain:** Magnanimous asks the learner to explain the idea in their own words.
4. **Build:** The learner writes, changes or configures something, rather than only watching.
5. **Test:** A quiz, coding test or experiment checks understanding.
6. **Reflect:** The learner explains one result, one limitation and one possible failure.
7. **Save evidence:** The system stores the submission, score, source and progress event.
8. **Review:** Magnanimous schedules the next lesson or a review of weak concepts.

The tutor must teach rather than simply complete the student's assignments. It should ask questions, provide hints, explain mistakes and require evidence before marking a practical outcome complete.

## 5. What students are actually buying

The following is the original Magnanimous service layer around the free source material:

- Initial placement assessment and personalized course plan.
- Persistent Magnanimous AI tutor with subject-specialist skills beneath the same central brain.
- Original lesson notes, simplified explanations and examples.
- Quizzes, practical challenges and spaced review.
- Lab instructions, starter projects, tests and grading rubrics authored by Magnanimous.
- Progress dashboard with lesson history, assessment results and completed evidence.
- Project feedback and portfolio organization.
- Course-completion certificates for eligible Magnanimous courses.
- Student support and account controls.

A student's learning record belongs to the student and must remain tenant-separated. The platform must never train a public or shared tutor on private student submissions without a separate, explicit and revocable permission.

## 6. Proposed subscription plan

These are **pilot price recommendations**, not live Stripe prices. Validate actual provider costs and the first students' willingness to pay before publishing the checkout.

| Plan | Proposed price | Included experience |
|---|---:|---|
| Open Campus | Free | Free source videos and course links, public learning maps, sample lessons, starter resources and placement preview |
| AI College Student | **₱399/month** | Guided pathways, complete source map, Magnanimous tutoring allowance, quizzes, tracked progress, review queue and course projects |
| AI College Builder | **₱999/month** | Everything in Student, plus advanced labs, larger measured tutoring allowance, project evaluations, capstone submissions, agent/MCP practice and a portfolio workspace |
| Campus / Organization | Quoted after discovery | Separate cohort administration, instructor/learner roles, organization reporting and agreed support scope |

### Subscription principles

- Free source material stays free. Do not charge people just to watch a creator's public video.
- No mandatory annual commitment at launch. Start with monthly plans and evaluate retention before adding annual pricing.
- No unlimited AI tutoring, agent execution, GPU usage or paid-provider use.
- The exact included AI allowance must be displayed before checkout and based on measured costs, not a made-up number of questions.
- Prefer local, browser, native and low-cost execution for beginner labs. Do not include cloud GPU time in a base subscription.
- When an allowance is exhausted, offer a free/native fallback or a clearly priced, prepaid top-up. Never silently create an unfunded provider expense.
- For verified direct third-party usage costs, follow the platform's existing rule: customer-funded charge = direct cost × 1.20. This is a 20% markup, not a guaranteed 20% profit margin.
- Do not change the existing Magnanimous Plus, CRM Pro, Studio, Business or other subscription prices as part of this product.
- Treat College as a separate education product. Existing Studio course-authoring and learning-asset features should remain distinct from the student's College subscription.

### Checkout and billing safeguards

Reuse the current Magnanimous Stripe Billing architecture rather than introducing another payment identity.

Before checkout, show the plan name, full PHP price, billing interval, renewal timing, included tutor allowance, exclusions, taxes where applicable, cancellation method and refund terms. Require affirmative acceptance of recurring-billing terms and record the accepted version and timestamp.

Stripe-confirmed payment—not a browser redirect—must activate access. Webhooks must be signature-verified, idempotent and tenant-scoped. Prevent duplicate subscriptions; preserve existing paid plans; provide Customer Portal cancellation and plan-management access; and revoke paid entitlements after confirmed expiration/cancellation according to the disclosed policy.

No live Stripe product, price, checkout link or customer charge is authorized by this document. Pilot prices become publishable only after cost review, owner approval and end-to-end billing tests.

## 7. Magnanimous AI teaching architecture

Magnanimous AI remains the central intelligence, identity, memory, reasoning, orchestration and verification layer. The college is a department/product of Magnanimous, not a new independent AI identity.

Recommended learning services beneath that brain:

- **Curriculum Router:** maps goals and placement results to source lessons.
- **Tutor:** explains, questions, hints and adapts difficulty.
- **Assessment Engine:** generates and grades quizzes against approved rubrics.
- **Lab Coach:** gives safe, step-by-step development guidance.
- **Project Reviewer:** tests submitted artifacts and checks evidence.
- **Learning Memory:** stores only the student's own authorized progress and learning preferences.
- **Certificate Service:** issues verifiable completion records for Magnanimous-created courses.
- **Safety and Cost Guard:** checks permissions, secrets, untrusted code and usage limits.

External models, video hosts, code execution providers and other tools are replaceable execution rails. They do not own the student's learning record or become Magnanimous's identity.

Never run arbitrary student code in the production worker or with platform credentials. Labs require an isolated, least-privilege execution environment, restricted network access, CPU/memory/time limits, no secrets by default, and clear cleanup.

## 8. Certificates and truthful claims

Magnanimous may issue a **Certificate of Completion** for its own published course when the student meets its published requirements and passes the assessments.

The college must not advertise:
- An accredited degree, university credit or government-recognized qualification.
- A certification issued by MIT, Stanford, Microsoft, Google, Hugging Face, Anthropic, the Linux Foundation or another external organization.
- An official partnership or endorsement without a written agreement.
- Guaranteed employment, income, job placement or exam success.

The public description must say that Magnanimous AI College is an independent online learning program and that its certificates document completion of Magnanimous coursework, not academic accreditation or third-party professional certification.
